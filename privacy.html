Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

The short version

This is a concept website, not a live care, emergency or medical service. It does not collect survey responses or contact details. Optional Google Analytics stays off unless you actively enable it, and you can change that choice at any time.

1. Who is responsible for this site

A-Z Care is an independent, pre-launch product project and is responsible for this concept website. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to azcare.project@gmail.com. Please do not include health information, precise location or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for the request. The controller details in this notice will be updated before any formal research, account system or live service is launched.

2. What this website stores

Essential local settings

The site uses your browser’s local storage to remember your privacy choice and settings inside the interactive phone demonstration, such as the selected widget and demo permission toggles. These values stay in your browser and are not submitted to A-Z Care. You can remove them by clearing this site’s browser data.

Optional analytics

If you select “Accept analytics” or enable analytics in Privacy settings, the site loads Google Analytics. It may process page views, approximate device and browser details, referrer information, coarse geographic information and randomly generated identifiers. Google Analytics may set cookies beginning with _ga. The analytics cookie lifetime is configured to 90 days on this site.

Analytics is used only to understand aggregate use of the prototype. Advertising storage, Google Signals and ad-personalisation signals are disabled. The site does not load Google Analytics before consent and does not use analytics if you reject it.

3. Legal basis and your choice

Optional analytics is processed only on the basis of your consent. Essential local storage is used to provide the privacy control and the interactive demonstration you request. Rejecting optional analytics does not reduce access to the website.

You may withdraw consent at any time by selecting “Privacy settings” in the footer and turning analytics off. This stops future analytics from this browser and removes A-Z Care analytics cookies that the page can access. Withdrawal does not undo processing that occurred while consent was active.

4. Service providers and international transfers

The prototype source code is stored in a private GitHub repository. When an access-controlled prototype is made available, its hosting provider may process technical request logs under its own terms and privacy practices. This notice will name the production host before research invitations are sent. When analytics is enabled, Google LLC provides Google Analytics and may process data outside your country. Those providers are responsible for the safeguards described in their applicable data-protection terms.

5. Retention

Local demo settings remain in your browser until you clear them. A saved analytics choice expires after 180 days, after which the site asks again. The analytics identifier cookie is configured to expire after 90 days. Analytics event retention also depends on the Google Analytics property settings; those settings must be reviewed and recorded before the site is used for formal product research.

6. Children

A-Z Care includes product ideas for children and families, but this website is intended for adults evaluating the concept. It does not knowingly request personal information from children. A parent or guardian should reject optional analytics when a child uses the prototype. Any future child-facing service will require separate safeguards, age-appropriate information and parental-consent design where legally required.

7. Your data-protection rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal data, withdraw consent and complain to a supervisory authority. Because this site does not collect names or account details, A-Z Care may not be able to connect an analytics identifier to a particular person without additional information from that person.

8. Future research and product features

This policy covers the current static concept website only. It does not yet cover a future survey, user account, mobile app, trusted-contact service, live location, health data or emergency workflow. A new notice and appropriate consent controls will be provided before any of those features collect real information.

9. Changes to this policy

This page may be updated as the prototype changes. The date at the top will show the latest revision. The saved choice includes a consent-version number. A new version is required after a material change, so an older analytics choice will be ignored and the site will ask again.
