A-Z CARE · PROJECT STATUS

OUR MISSION

Technology for peace of mind.

A-Z Care explores how a connected phone, watch and trusted-contact experience could make digital safety, everyday care and emergency awareness easier to understand.

Try the interactive prototype → Explore the ecosystem

CURRENT STATUS

A working demonstration, not a finished device.

The website contains interactive phone and watch interfaces and scripted safety scenarios. These illustrate the proposed user experience, not deployed emergency, medical or anti-fraud services.

AVAILABLE TO EXPLORE

Interactive web prototype

Try the phone and watch interfaces, scripted safety screens and simulated SOS flows using fictional example data.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Privacy by default

Permission-based sharing and transparent choices are product goals. Optional website analytics are governed by consent controls.

NOT YET AVAILABLE

Real-world protection services

The prototype does not call emergency services, measure real health data, monitor real threats or send real trusted-contact alerts.

DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

Build the experience. Validate the safety. Earn trust.

The roadmap below describes development priorities, not committed release dates.

01 · PROTOTYPE

Explore and refine

Improve accessibility, mobile layouts, device navigation and clear explanations of simulated behavior.

02 · VALIDATION

Test the safety workflows

Evaluate consent, emergency escalation, offline handling and usability with realistic but non-sensitive test scenarios.

03 · INTEGRATION RESEARCH

Assess hardware and services

Investigate viable watch, connectivity and sensor integrations alongside security, privacy and regulatory requirements.

A-Z Care is a concept and interactive web prototype. It is not an emergency service, a medical device or a finished consumer phone.
