AI-ASSISTED FAMILY SAFETY

Protection that feels simple.

A-Z Care is a privacy-first smartphone safety concept for kids, seniors and the people who care for them — combining scam awareness, SOS, family safety and an AI assistant in one clear experience.

ONE SAFETY LAYER

Useful when everything is fine.
Ready when it isn’t.

A-Z Care focuses on a few understandable safety flows rather than overwhelming users with dozens of controls.

SOS

Emergency SOS

Quickly alert trusted contacts, share the device location and show a clear emergency status.

◇

Scam awareness

Surface suspicious patterns in calls, messages and links before a user feels pressured to act.

⌖

Family safety

Consent-based location, safe zones, battery and offline status for people who choose to share it.

✦

AI Safety Assistant

Explain suspicious situations in plain language and suggest safer next steps without pretending to be infallible.

A-Z CARE AI

Not sure what to trust? Ask before you act.

The assistant is designed to help interpret suspicious texts, links and calls. It explains warning signs, recommends safer actions and can involve a trusted family member when the user wants a second opinion.

DIFFERENT NEEDS · ONE SYSTEM

Designed around the person using it.

KIDS MODE

Safety without turning the phone into a cage.

Safe browsing, age-appropriate controls, optional screen-time rules, safe zones and emergency access.

School mode ONEmergency calls ALWAYSGames during school PAUSED
SENIOR MODE

Less complexity. Bigger confidence.

Large controls, trusted contacts, voice assistance, scam warnings and an obvious SOS path.

PRIVACY FIRST

Safety should not require invisible tracking.

The concept is built around clear consent, visible permissions and the ability to switch sharing off. A family-safety product should explain what it knows and why.

A-Z Care is currently a concept/prototype. These controls demonstrate the intended product philosophy, not a live security service.
