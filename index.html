WHY A-Z CARE

Smartphones are powerful. Safety is still fragmented.

People often need several apps, settings and services to handle scams, family safety, health routines and emergencies. A-Z Care explores what happens when those needs are designed as one understandable experience.

Scams

Suspicious messages, links and callers are hard to judge quickly.

Falls

A possible fall matters most when nobody nearby knows something happened.

Health routines

Medication, glucose and blood-pressure tracking can become another complicated task.

Family safety

Families need useful alerts and trusted contacts without hidden surveillance.

Complexity

Many phones expect the person to adapt to the device instead of the other way around.

WHAT IT DOES

One phone. Safety that adapts to you.

The interactive prototype combines digital protection, AI Care, family and location tools, trusted contacts, everyday health support, fall detection, SOS, privacy controls and device information.

Protect & AI Care

Check suspicious messages, links and callers. AI Care explains warning signs and safer next steps in simple language.

Health & Fall Detection

Health demos include diabetes logging, medication and blood-pressure concepts. Fall Detection can ask if you are OK and escalate to trusted contacts when there is no response.

Family, Location & Contacts

Trusted contacts, consent-based location, family status and meaningful safety events without hidden tracking.

SOS & Privacy

Emergency escalation can share status and last available location with trusted contacts. Privacy and sharing controls stay visible and adjustable.

PRIVACY & TRUST

Built for safety. Designed for privacy.

A safety product should explain what it uses, why it uses it and who can receive information. These are design principles for the A-Z Care concept, not claims of completed certification.

Visible controlsPrivacy permissions, safety alerts and sharing settings stay understandable.
Consent firstFamily and location sharing are explicit and controllable.
Minimum necessary dataUse only what a feature needs, especially for location, health and emergency functions.

A-Z Care is currently a concept and interactive web prototype. It is not an emergency service, medical device or finished consumer phone.

FUTURE CONCEPT · A-Z CARE ECOSYSTEM

Phone + Watch + Health Guardian + resilient connectivity

The wider A-Z Care vision connects a phone, Wear OS watch, health sensors and trusted people through one privacy-first safety layer. Research areas include fall and no-response detection, heart and oxygen trends, ECG on compatible hardware, sleep, temperature and breathing context, CGM and blood-pressure device integration, UWB precise finding, offline emergency beacons, low-bandwidth safety connectivity and encrypted nearby relay / mesh concepts.

A-Z Sensor Hub is planned as the common data layer so supported CGM, wearable and medical-device integrations do not spread vendor-specific logic across the product. Core safety logic is intended to remain useful locally even when cloud connectivity is unavailable.
