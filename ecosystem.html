TECHNOLOGY VISION · RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

A-Z Care
Ecosystem.

A privacy-first safety platform connecting the phone, watch, health sensors, trusted people and multiple communication paths. The goal is simple: useful protection should keep working even when a person is stressed, offline or unable to ask for help.

Product direction, not a shipping specification. Health features may require compatible or certified hardware, regulatory validation and user consent. A-Z Care is not currently a medical or emergency service.

Resilient Connectivity

Normal pathMobile data / SIM / eSIM → Wi-Fi → available internet
Safety connectivityResearch partner vSIM/eSIM connectivity for low-bandwidth A-Z safety traffic
Nearby relayBluetooth LE / Wi-Fi Direct / Wi-Fi Aware research for encrypted device-to-device emergency relay
Store & forwardCarry an encrypted emergency packet until a participating device reaches connectivity
Mesh R&DExplore multi-hop emergency delivery without exposing message contents to relay devices
SatelliteFuture integration where device hardware, OS and regional services support satellite emergency communication
Design goalA-Z Care cannot promise connectivity everywhere. The system should attempt the safest available path to deliver critical information instead of assuming the internet is always present.

Lost Person ModeLast-known position · direction · battery · locally stored route breadcrumbs

Crash / impact R&DExplore strong-motion and context signals with confirmation before emergency escalation
