PRIVACY-FIRST FAMILY SAFETY

One connected experience for everyday safety.

A-Z Care explores how scam protection, family check-ins, wearable signals and emergency workflows could work together without turning safety into surveillance.

Explore the interactive prototype →

Prototype only. No real alerts, medical monitoring or emergency dispatch. This page is unlisted, not access-controlled.

01 / PROBLEM

Fragmented protection

People navigate separate apps and services for suspicious messages, care routines and urgent situations.

02 / APPROACH

Connected safety

A phone and wearable share explicit, consent-aware simulated safety events and understandable actions.

03 / PRODUCT

Interactive concept

Explore a working web prototype with a phone, watch, scam-review journey, SOS countdown and offline retry.

04 / PRIVACY

Consent by design

Location sharing is opt-in. Prototype events remain local to the browser and do not contact emergency services.

DEVELOPMENT PRIORITIES

From prototype to validated product

  1. Complete accessibility, interaction and security testing.
  2. Conduct user research with caregivers and intended users.
  3. Validate technical feasibility and privacy requirements.
  4. Scope an Android MVP and controlled pilot.
